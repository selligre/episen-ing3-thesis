\begin{posterbox}[name=problematique, span=2, column=0, row=0]{Problem}
	Kernel-level anti-cheats (Ring 0) are the industry standard for stopping cheaters in competitive FPS games — but they require granting a driver unrestricted access to the entire system, not just the game. This creates three concrete risks:
	\begin{itemize}
		\item Security: expands the attack surface
		\item Privacy: violates GDPR principles, can read any process in memory
		\item Trust: drives boycotts, excludes all users but Windows'
	\end{itemize}
